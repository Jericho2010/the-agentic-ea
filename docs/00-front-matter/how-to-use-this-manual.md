## Suggested sequence (first pass)

1. Part I — understand Orbus as practiced (capabilities + 20 scenarios).
2. Part II — ladders (do not skip the four-column matrix).
3. Part III — ANEA operating model (intended vs observed; control plane).
4. Skim Part IV index, then deep-read the four pilot scenarios: S01, S05, S08, S16.
5. Part V — platform backbone (especially fact-check notes).
6. Part VI — metamodel (PAM)—this is the connective tissue.
7. Part VII — roadmap.

## How chapters are structured

- Baseline chapters always start from **Orbus Infinity**.
- Playbooks always include Agent-native, Databricks, and Snowflake patterns.
- Verdicts use **control-plane language** (enforce / analytics / render), not exclusive “winner takes all,” unless a clear enforcement locus exists.
- Every chapter ends with **Citations** and a **validation checklist**.

## Confidence markers

Look for `confidence: high|medium|low` on product-specific claims. Low-confidence items are hypotheses to verify against your Orbus tenant version and current vendor docs.

## What not to do

- Do not treat illustrative SQL/Python as production-ready.
- Do not invent API names; see [Fact-check notes](../06-platform-backbone/08-factcheck-notes.md).
- Do not confuse Archify (compile/verify diagrams) with a full EA repository.
