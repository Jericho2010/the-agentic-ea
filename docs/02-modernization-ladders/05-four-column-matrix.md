## How to read

- Moving **Legacy→Earth** removes license lock but may reduce governance UX.
- Moving **Earth→Moon** is where developer adoption usually jumps.
- Moving **Moon→Mars** should be staged; pilot Moon first.
- Lakehouse backbone (Part V) typically accelerates C4, C6, C10, C12, C16-class analytics even when enforcement stays on Moon.
