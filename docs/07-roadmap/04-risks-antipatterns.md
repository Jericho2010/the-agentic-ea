# 7.4 — Risks, anti-patterns, and what not to automate

## Anti-patterns

1. Recreating Orbus inside Confluence with agents pasting PNGs.
2. Lakehouse-only EA with no CI enforcement.
3. Archify showcase compiles on every commit for huge graphs (cost/noise).
4. Invented vendor APIs in “reference architectures.”
5. Skipping aliases/evidence → poisoned CMDB joins.
6. Automating ARB approvals without human accountability for material risk.

## What not to automate (yet)

- Final risk acceptance for regulated changes
- Legal interpretations of licenses/contracts
- M&A clean-room policy exceptions
